# Progress

## Phase 0
Multi-loader skeleton, CC0, CI, and the plain-Java recovery ladder (25/50/75/95%). Merged.

## Phase 3
The track-laying TBM is a moving contraption: it rides forward with the face, lines with cobble or mined stone (crafted segments optional), and lays a smooth 1-in-2 ramp. Pumped water fills the quench tank. A full muck buffer pauses it until a train hauls the rock away. Haulage is a rectifier, locomotive, cars, dump, and block signals. There is still no Field Manual page.

## Phase 2
True-size crushing circuit (grizzly, jaw, 6 m belt, screen, cone, ball mill, cyclones) and flotation bank (4 roughers,
2 scavengers, 2 cleaners, blower, thickener, filter) on Wildcatter motors. Fines are the mill product. A full bank pays
the 75% route for sulfides and for vanilla copper and gold. Lime is calcined calcite. Quicklime, xanthate, MIBC and copper
sulfate are Tailings items; Wildcatter has no lime.

## Phase 1
Hand-cranked stamp mill, hand screen, shaking table and magnetic separator. Country rock, the 27 Wildcatter ores, and vanilla
iron, copper and gold have size items and mineral lists. Gravity and magnetic concentration pay 50%. Nine Tailings nuggets
craft into one mineral item; iron and gold use the vanilla nuggets.

The starter line's game tests still cover the stamp mill and the magnetic separator. There is still no Field Manual page.
