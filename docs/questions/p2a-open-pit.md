# Open pit P2a — decided while building (2026-11-09)

The plan's numbers were approved by Tim on 2026-11-09. These were added while building it, each because a test found the plan did not
work as written (PROGRESS.md, "Open pit P2a").

### 1. Turning round at the stake before an end
- **Chosen:** the truck stops at the turn stake and turns round on full lock at 2 m/s until its nose is within 15 degrees of pointing
  away from the end, then backs in. It gives up after 60 s ("give it 25 m of open ground").
- **Why:** a truck mostly reaches the stake before the dump facing the dump, so it cannot back straight in; real dumps have a turning
  area for exactly this.
- **Alternatives:** make the player lay the road as a Y so no turning is needed (the first plan: hard to lay right, and the test
  showed it); or have the truck back round a reversing arc (works but needs more room and looks odd).

### 2. Steering on a point 15 m ahead along its lane
- **Chosen:** 15 m.
- **Why:** steering at the far stake's lane point left trucks off their lane for most of a leg, and opposing trucks stopped for each
  other. 15 m is about one and a half truck lengths: quick into lane without weaving.
- **Alternatives:** 10 m (tighter, weaves more at speed) or 25 m (smoother, slower into lane).

### 3. Waiting 40 m short of a busy one-way leg
- **Chosen:** 40 m.
- **Why:** waiting at the stake blocked the truck coming off the leg. 40 m (four truck lengths) keeps the junction clear.
- **Alternatives:** 25 m (tighter junctions) or a WAIT stake the player places.

### 4. Backing on past a DUMP POCKET stake to the bumper
- **Chosen:** up to 3 m past the stake, until the bumper stops it.
- **Why:** with the stake short of the bumper the hopper was out of reach of the tail; real trucks back until their wheels touch the
  bumper.
- **Alternatives:** require the stake exactly at the bumper (fussy), or search further (risks backing into a pit with no bumper).

### 5. Waiting at a dry fuel bay
- **Chosen:** 2 minutes, then it goes on and reads "no fuel at the bay".
- **Alternatives:** wait forever (a fleet can stall at an empty bay), or skip straight away.
